The correction is not a per-judge z-score. A z-score divides by a standard deviation that is zero for a judge who scored everything the same, and it is meaningless for a judge with one ballot. Instead the scale is shrunk toward the pool, σ̃ = (nσ + kσpool)/(n + k) with k = {{ result.params.k_shrink }}, so it can never be zero; and the bias is fitted by a ridge-penalised additive model with λjudge = {{ result.params.lambda_judge }} and λproject = {{ result.params.lambda_project }}, so a judge with one ballot has only {{ '%.0f' % (100 / (1 + result.params.lambda_judge)) }}% of their residual attributed to bias instead of all of it. Residual sd after the fit is {{ '%.4f' % result.residual_sd }}. Full derivation and a worked example: proof artefact.
